## Direction contract

THESIS: PlateBeam is the live restaurant operating system — every table, kitchen station, guest phone, and owner ledger synchronized in real time on a high-contrast Obsidian command surface.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian (`#09090B`), Carbon (`#111115`), and Graphite (`#18181F`) command panels with subtle `36px` `.grid-bg` texture, Beam Orange (`#FF5412`) primary CTAs (`shadow-beam`), Inter (`400–900`) typography + JetBrains Mono (`400–700`) telemetry, and `rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl` soft-command geometry. Guest QR surface (`/t/[qrToken]`) transitions to Warm Cream (`#FAF7F2`) for high-legibility dining.

STORY: Visitor understands synchronized multi-surface restaurant OS in seconds, sees the live channel architecture (Guest QR, FOH POS, Kitchen KDS, Owner BI, Platform Admin), and launches any surface or staff login immediately.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Grid-textured Obsidian canvas; PlateBeam OS mark and headline left; concise zero-app sync value proposition; primary Beam Orange CTA “Open Guest Channel” + secondary “Staff Console” / “Live Demo”; surface channels as clean interactive rows with clear role & latency telemetry.

FINISH: Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; verify against `DESIGN.md`, `DESIGN_DIFF_REPORT.md`, and WCAG 2.2 AA contrast & `44×44px` touch target standards.
